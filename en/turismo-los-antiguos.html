Half-day trips

Viewpoints

Different views of the valley, its two rivers (Los Antiguos and Jeinimeni) and the best sunsets. Distances from the visitor center:

  • Mirador Uendeunk ("Good Spirit"), via the former Route 520: 900 m. View over the town.
  • Lake Buenos Aires viewpoint, via the former Old Pier: 2.3 km.
  • Jeinimeni River viewpoint, on the way to Provincial Route 41: 2.8 km.
  • Valley viewpoint (Mirador del Valle), via the former Route 520: 3.5 km. View of the farmland and the mountain ranges.

Farms (chacras)

Depending on the season, you can visit family farms to taste their fresh and homemade products, pick cherries, strawberries or raspberries, or walk among the orchards in white bloom in October.

Laguna de los Juncos Urban Nature Reserve

A natural area in the heart of town that protects native plants (neneo, paramela, calafate) and shelters more than 60 resident and migratory bird species, such as flamingos, black-necked swans, herons and plovers. Great for birdwatching, photography and trails.

Full-day trips

Scenic Route 41

A gravel mountain road that climbs from 200 to 1,500 m above sea level at El Portezuelo, along the canyons of the Jeinimeni and Los Antiguos rivers, through an 800-hectare lenga forest and volcanic landscapes. Los Antiguos–forest: 55 km; Los Antiguos–Portezuelo: 77 km; Portezuelo–Lago Posadas: 35 km. Suitable for any vehicle (high-clearance recommended); heavy snow or rain can make it impassable. Fill up in Los Antiguos and check road conditions before you go.

La Ascensión Wildlife Reserve

17 km away on National Route 43: the historic buildings of one of the region's largest sheep ranches and hiking circuits of varying difficulty; the longest climbs to the Lake Buenos Aires plateau (1,456 m), with high-altitude lagoons where the hooded grebe nests. Part of Patagonia National Park (52,811 ha).

Cueva de las Manos

220 km from Los Antiguos: rock paintings nearly 10,000 years old, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. There are three ways in:

  • Access 1 (includes a demanding hike): 115 km of paved road (about 1 h 20 min) and 18 km of gravel to the park entrance, plus a 2 km hike.
  • Access 2 (4x4 only): 147 km of paved road (about 1 h 40 min) and 28 km of gravel.
  • Access 3 (suitable for all): 184 km of paved road (about 2 h) and 47 km of gravel.

Ask at the visitor center for details.

Fishing

In Lake Buenos Aires and the Los Antiguos and Jeinimeni rivers: brown, rainbow and brook trout, Creole perch, and Sakura and Coho salmon. Fishing is possible year-round; the best periods are August to November and March to April.

Trips into Chile

With Los Antiguos as your base: Chile Chico is about 7 km away; from there, Laguna del Jeinimeni National Reserve (60 km south), Bahía Jara (about 25 km) and the Marble Caves (about 174 km), which can be visited as a day trip before returning to Los Antiguos for the night.
