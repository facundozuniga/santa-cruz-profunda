Home › Articles › Tres Lagos and Lago Cardiel: what to do in the heart of Santa Cruz’s Ruta 40

By Santa Cruz Profunda, from Puerto San Julián · Updated 2026-10-30

Tres Lagos is a small town on National Route 40, in the middle of the steppe, between El Calafate and Gobernador Gregores. Most travelers pass through, fill up and keep going. If you have a spare day, it is worth stopping: nearby there is a rock the Tehuelche considered sacred, rock shelters with paintings, a petrified forest and two huge lakes that few people reach. The short town page is at Tres Lagos.

Further north, already in the Río Chico department, lies Lago Cardiel: a turquoise lake in the middle of the plateau, with no town on its shore, known among anglers for its trout and for a story about fingerlings that supposedly fell from a plane. Both are on the same road: if you are driving Ruta 40 toward northern Santa Cruz, they are on your way.

Where they are and how to get there

Tres Lagos is in the Lago Argentino department, on the banks of the Shehuen or Chalía River, about 230 m above sea level according to Turismo Ruta 40 (Wikipedia gives 226 m). Its name comes from the lakes you can reach from town: Viedma, Tar and San Martín.

The nearest airport is El Calafate. According to Turismo Santa Cruz, it is about 2 hours from there on Ruta 40.

The figures come from Turismo Ruta 40 and Wikipedia, which do not fully agree; that is why we give a range.

To reach Lago Cardiel, take RN 40 to the junction with RP 29. According to the Vialidad Provincial road report, from Tres Lagos it is 37 km of pavement to the La Lucía ranch and then 44 + 29 km of gravel to that junction: about 110 km in total. From Gobernador Gregores it is about 70 km west on RP 29, according to Welcome Argentina and Interpatagonia; Welcome places the lake 12 km from the junction with RN 40.

Ruta 40 conditions on this stretch

Between Tres Lagos and the Cardiel junction there are still about 73 km of gravel, the most dreaded stretch of Ruta 40 in the province. Traveler reports on Ruta0 mention loose stones and, after rain, mud. In winter it gets seriously difficult: in the Vialidad Provincial report of August 2026 the stretch was listed with snow, 4x4 vehicles only and chains mandatory. Conditions change a lot: check before you travel at Vialidad Provincial de Santa Cruz (AGVP) – Parte de transitabilidad de rutas nacionales. More detail in our guide to Ruta 40 in Santa Cruz: fuel, gravel and distances.

Other roads leaving town:

  • RN 288: toward Comandante Luis Piedra Buena, on Ruta 3. It is gravel. More about that destination in Piedra Buena and Monte León.
  • RP 31: gravel toward the Shekaiken lagoon, Lago Tar and Lago San Martín. According to Turismo Ruta 40, it can only be driven in a regular car in summer.

A bit of history

The area was Aonikenk (southern Tehuelche) territory. The town began as a stop for the ox carts that hauled wool from the Andean ranches to Piedra Buena and came back with supplies. In 1925 Fausto Vallina set up a blacksmith’s shop, the first settlement; the place was first called Piedra Clavada. In 1937 it was recognized as a town under the name Tres Lagos, and on August 3, 1973 it was officially founded by provincial law: its anniversary is celebrated that day. The 2022 census recorded 408 inhabitants, according to Wikipedia.

Lago Cardiel is named after the Jesuit José Cardiel, who in 1745 set out with Matías Strobel and José Quiroga from the bay of San Julián toward the interior. Lakes Strobel and Quiroga honor his companions. If that history interests you, see also what to do in Puerto San Julián.

What to do in Tres Lagos

Piedra Clavada

It is a rock formation over 20 m tall that looks as if it had been driven vertically into the ground, overlooking the Shehuen River valley, according to Turismo Santa Cruz. Turismo Ruta 40 places it on RN 288, about 4 km east of town, north of the Chalía River after crossing the bridge. The same guide quotes historian Mario Echeverría Baleta: according to old settlers, the Tehuelche gathered around the rock once a year to call on their gods.

Chalía River rock paintings

On RP 31, at km 21 and before the bridge over the Chalía River, there is a rock shelter with painted hands of different sizes. Turismo Ruta 40 says you reach it by walking about 1,100 m along the south bank of the river, upstream, to a rock wall at the foot of the bluff. Turismo Santa Cruz also mentions petroglyphs in the area. Look but do not touch: damaging or removing archaeological remains is a crime. To find out whether it is best to go with a guide, ask at the town’s Tourism Office (details below).

Petrified forest

About 20 km southeast of town there is a petrified forest that Turismo Santa Cruz presents as the southernmost in South America, some 90 million years old. We found no official information on how to get there or whether a guide is required: ask at the Tourism Office before you go.

Lago Tar and Shekaiken lagoon

Heading north on RP 31 you pass the Shekaiken lagoon and, further on, Lago Tar. According to Turismo Ruta 40, the Tar is shallow, covers more than 35 km² and has muddy water: its Tehuelche name means “dirty”. The same guide tells a legend of “monsters” on its shores, recounted by historian Manuel Llarás Samitier.

Lago San Martín

It is shared with Chile, where it is called O'Higgins. According to Turismo Ruta 40 it covers 1,013 km², of which 459 km² are Argentine, and its O'Higgins arm reaches 836 m deep. At the fork of RP 31 and RP 33 you can see Cerro Kach Aike, an old volcanic neck 1,008 m high.

The loop to lakes Tar and San Martín is more than 240 km round trip, all gravel and with no fuel. On RP 33, along the south shore of the lake, the road ends about 120 km from town at Estancia El Cóndor, which offers trekking, horseback riding, outings and fishing. Turismo Ruta 40 recommends confirming your booking before leaving if you plan to stay at a ranch.

Chalía River and community campground

The river flows all year round and, according to Wikipedia, there is a community campground on its bank. We found no official information on its facilities or rates: ask at the Tourism Office.

What to do at Lago Cardiel

Sport fishing

It is the main reason to go. According to Welcome Argentina, there are rainbow trout, brook trout and landlocked salmon, of very good size. The lake covers about 350 km² according to Spanish Wikipedia (370 km² according to the English version) and is up to 76 m deep. At the head of the lake, Interpatagonia says, you can see black-necked swans and flamingos.

To fish you need a provincial permit. Seasons, fees and regulations: check before you travel at Pesca Santa Cruz (provincia) – Permisos de pesca, temporada 2026/2027.

Salmon Festival

According to Welcome Argentina, every Easter Week the Sports Department of the Municipality of Gobernador Gregores holds the Fiesta del Salmón at the lake, with individual, team and youth categories. We have not yet found the official date for the 2027 edition: ask Gobernador Gregores Tourism.

The legend of the plane

Anglers tell this story, recorded by Welcome Argentina and Interpatagonia: in the 1940s, a plane carrying fingerlings to Lake Fagnano, in Tierra del Fuego, was caught in a storm and the pilot released them into the first body of water he saw, the Cardiel. It is a tale with no documentation to confirm it.

When to go

Turismo Ruta 40 recommends the area between November and April, the warmest season. The climate is cool, dry and windy, with westerly winds, and in winter there are frosts, ice and occasional snowfall. According to Wikipedia, in Tres Lagos January averages 13 °C (with exceptional highs of 28 °C) and July 1 °C, with lows down to −22 °C. Near Lago San Martín, Turismo Ruta 40 gives summer highs of up to 25 °C and nights of 10 to 15 °C. Bring warm clothes even in summer.

How many days to stay

  • Half a day: Piedra Clavada and the Chalía paintings, on your way along Ruta 40.
  • A full day: add the petrified forest or Lago Tar.
  • Two days: the loop to Lago San Martín, staying the night in town or at a ranch.
  • Lago Cardiel: a day trip from Gobernador Gregores; longer if you are going to fish.

These are our own suggestions.

Travel tips

  • Fuel: according to Turismo Ruta 40 there is fuel in El Calafate, Tres Lagos and Gobernador Gregores. Tres Lagos has a YPF station. Always fill up.
  • No services at the Cardiel: there is no town on the shore; Interpatagonia mentions only rough fishermen’s shelters. According to Wikipedia, the lake water is not fit for drinking: bring your own.
  • Bring: water, food, a paper map and warm clothes, as Turismo Ruta 40 recommends.
  • Cell signal: Wikipedia says phone service in town is limited. We found no official coverage data: expect to have no signal on RP 31 and on the gravel toward the Cardiel.
  • Gravel: slow down, carry a spare tire in good condition and ease off when passing another vehicle to avoid stones hitting your windshield.
  • Tres Lagos Tourism Office: Av. San Martín s/n, Monday to Friday 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., tel. +54 9 2966 342484, turismotreslagos@gmail.com, according to Turismo Santa Cruz. Opening hours and guided visits: check before you travel at Turismo Santa Cruz – Tres Lagos.

According to Wikipedia and Turismo Ruta 40, the town has a bakery, a butcher’s, two grocery stores, a restaurant, cabins, an inn, a health post and a tire repair shop.

To continue your trip: Gobernador Gregores, Bajo Caracoles, El Chaltén, El Calafate and our guide to Ruta 40 in Santa Cruz.

Frequently asked questions

Is there fuel in Tres Lagos?

Yes, there is a YPF station on Ruta 40. Turismo Ruta 40 describes the town as the only service center between El Calafate and Gobernador Gregores: fill up the tank.

Can you drive to Lago Cardiel in a regular car?

It depends on the gravel. Between Tres Lagos and the Cardiel junction there are about 73 km of gravel which, according to traveler reports, has loose stones and mud after rain, and in winter the road authority has opened it to 4x4 vehicles only. Check the Vialidad Provincial road report before you set out.

How far is Lago Cardiel from Tres Lagos?

About 110 km on Ruta 40 to the junction with RP 29, according to the Vialidad Provincial road report, plus about 12 km to the lake, according to Welcome Argentina.

Where to stay near Lago Cardiel?

In Gobernador Gregores, about 70 km away, the nearest place to stock up, or in Tres Lagos. There is no lodging on the lakeshore: only rough fishermen’s shelters.

What is the Piedra Clavada?

A rock formation over 20 m tall that looks as if it were stuck into the ground, about 4 km east of Tres Lagos on RN 288. It had ceremonial value for the Tehuelche.
