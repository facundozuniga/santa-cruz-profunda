Home › Articles › National Cherry Festival in Los Antiguos: a practical guide

By Santa Cruz Profunda, from Puerto San Julián · Updated 2026-11-07

Every January, Los Antiguos goes from a quiet town to the host of one of the biggest festivals in Santa Cruz. The National Cherry Festival celebrates the harvest in the valley of Argentina’s National Cherry Capital, on the southern shore of Lake Buenos Aires and 7 km from Chile Chico, according to Turismo Ruta 40.

There are shows, a horse-riding contest, a crafts and producers’ fair, the queen’s election and, above all, cherries. The 35th edition took place from January 9 to 11, 2026, with free admission, and according to municipal figures released by the Santa Cruz Ministry of Production, the first day drew more than 4,000 people.

Here is when it happens, what you will find, how to get there and what to book early. For the town in general, see our guide to things to do in Los Antiguos and the Los Antiguos page.

When is the National Cherry Festival

It is held every January, once the harvest is over, according to Argentina’s Chamber of Deputies. These are some editions with published dates:

YearEditionDates
201425.ªJanuary 9 to 12 (Welcome Argentina)
202333.ªJanuary (Chamber of Deputies)
202534.ªJanuary 10 to 12 (PAD)
202635.ªJanuary 9 to 11, Friday to Sunday (La Prensa de Santa Cruz and La Vanguardia Noticias)

As of November 2, 2026, we found no official date for the 2027 edition. For 2026, mayor Zulma Neira confirmed the dates in early September, according to TiempoSur, because that is when bookings start. Check the date with the Los Antiguos Tourism Office before you travel.

A bit of history

According to a draft declaration submitted to the Chamber of Deputies in 2023, the celebrations began in 1988, organized by residents and the municipality to mark the end of the picking season. The Fiestas Nacionales website and TiempoSur say 1991, so the year of the first edition varies by source. Over the years it grew into a popular festival with provincial reach and national standing.

Cherry trees arrived in the valley in the 1970s, from Mendoza, says Bichos de Campo. The same Chamber of Deputies draft mentions about 260 hectares in production and around 1,100 tonnes of cherries. In April 2025, Resolution 49/2025 of the Secretariat of Agriculture, Livestock and Fisheries recognized the Designation of Origin “Cereza del Valle de Los Antiguos – Patagonia”, which Bichos de Campo describes as the country’s fifth.

What’s on at the festival

Live music

Every night there is a stage with local, regional and national artists. In 2026 the opening night started on Friday at 9 p.m. with the official opening and local dance academies, and on Saturday the line-up included Los del Porsiquede, Jairo y la Changada and La Furia Cuartetera, among others, according to La Vanguardia Noticias. In 2025 the first night featured La 2001, says PAD. The line-up changes every year: check the 2027 one when it is announced.

Horse-riding contest and rodeo

The bronc riding is one of the highlights. In 2026 the Desafío de Tropillas was held on Saturday 10 and Sunday 11, with more than 5 million pesos in prizes (La Vanguardia Noticias), at the Clodomiro Inayado rodeo ground; InterPatagonia notes it was charged separately from the free festival. For January 2024, TiempoSur reported five riding categories: recado completo, basto con encimera, grupa, clina and cuero tendido. Bring warm layers for the afternoon, because the wind is relentless.

Crafts and producers’ fair

The Crafts and Producers’ Fair is the heart of the festival, with a crafts walk and a beer garden. The most sought-after items are cherry preserves, jams and cakes, according to PAD, and the Fiestas Nacionales website mentions tastings of cherry recipes. Before the 2026 edition, the mayor stressed that local food stalls keep prices affordable (Raíces Digital).

The queen’s election

The election of the Cherry Queen is one of the central moments of the festival, according to PAD.

Lake activities and things for kids

According to the Fiestas Nacionales website, the programme includes fishing contests, water activities, beach games, a mini kayak school, kite contests, skateboarding, hip-hop and murga troupes. Activities change every edition: check the programme with the Tourism Office.

Visiting the farms

The festival is the perfect excuse to tour the chacras (small farms). They are small, family-run places that, according to the Río Negro newspaper, sell fresh fruit, preserves, liqueurs, pickles, chocolates, chutneys and tarts, and at some you can pick your own berries. The Fiestas Nacionales website mentions a route through thirteen farms with tastings of homemade preserves and berry cakes during the festival. Which farms open and their hours change every season: ask at the Tourism Office.

The valley’s main varieties are Bing and Lapins, and in recent years late-harvest varieties such as Sweetheart, Kordia, Regina and Skeena have been added. According to the Designation of Origin protocol cited by Bichos de Campo, the harvest runs from late December to mid-February, so there is fresh fruit at the festival.

How to get to Los Antiguos

Los Antiguos is in northwestern Santa Cruz. You get there on Provincial Route 43, which links it with Perito Moreno, on Route 40: 58 km according to the province (59 km according to Turismo Ruta 40). From Comodoro Rivadavia, home to the nearest airport, you also arrive on RP 43. For the Route 40 sections, see our guide to Route 40 in Santa Cruz.

We found no up-to-date official information on bus companies and schedules, or on special services for the festival: ask at the Tourism Office.

If you are coming from Chile, the crossing is the Río Jeinimeni Pass, from Chile Chico. In previous editions, extended border hours were reported during the festival (check before you travel at Ahora Calafate – Fiesta Nacional de la Cereza (paso fronterizo con horario ampliado)); the 2027 hours still need to be confirmed. Requirements and tips in our guide to the Jeinimeni Pass.

Where to stay

This is the trickiest part. Ahead of the 2026 edition, the mayor spoke of hotel occupancy close to 100% and said many residents had added rooms for rent (Raíces Digital). Bookings for campsites, cabins and hotels start as soon as the date is confirmed, according to TiempoSur.

  • Book months in advance, especially if you are travelling with family.
  • Camping: Turismo Ruta 40 places the Municipal Campsite at the entrance to town, facing the lake. Ask the Tourism Office whether extra spots are opened for the festival.
  • Stay in Perito Moreno, 58 km away, and drive in each day.
  • Rooms in local homes: ask the Tourism Office what is available.

Accommodation and local businesses in the Los Antiguos directory.

How many days to stay

For the festival itself, plan on the three days of the weekend. To make the most of the trip, add a day to tour the farms at a relaxed pace, another for the Cueva de las Manos via Perito Moreno and another for Route 41 to Lago Posadas or to cross into Chile Chico. With four or five days you get a well-rounded trip. These are our suggestions.

Festival tips

  • Book accommodation as soon as possible: it is the first thing to sell out.
  • Fill up in every town: northwestern Santa Cruz has long stretches without fuel stations.
  • Bring warm clothes: January days are long, but nights and the wind can be cold.
  • Sun protection: the steppe sun is strong even when it is not hot.
  • Payments: we found no official information on ATMs or payment methods at the fair. Bring some cash and a backup option.
  • If you continue to Chile: cherries and other fresh fruit must be declared to the SAG and may be confiscated. Eat them before crossing.
  • Los Antiguos Tourism Office: Lago Buenos Aires 59, WhatsApp +54 9 297 621-2055, turismolosantiguos@gmail.com, every day from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.

Frequently asked questions

When is the 2027 National Cherry Festival?

As of November 2, 2026, we found no official date. It is held in January: in 2026 it ran from the 9th to the 11th and in 2025 from the 10th to the 12th. Confirm with the Los Antiguos Tourism Office.

Is admission free?

In 2026 admission to the festival was free; the Desafío de Tropillas (horse-riding contest) was charged separately. The 2027 arrangements have not been announced yet.

Where is Los Antiguos?

In northwestern Santa Cruz, on the southern shore of Lake Buenos Aires, 58 km from Perito Moreno on Provincial Route 43 and 7 km from Chile Chico.

Are there fresh cherries at the festival?

Yes. According to the Designation of Origin protocol, the harvest runs from late December to mid-February, so there is fresh fruit in January.

Can you go without a car?

The nearest airport is Comodoro Rivadavia. For buses to Los Antiguos and transfers to the farms, check companies and schedules with the Tourism Office.
