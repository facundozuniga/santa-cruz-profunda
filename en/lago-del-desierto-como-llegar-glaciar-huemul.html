Home › Articles › Lago del Desierto: how to get there from El Chaltén, Huemul glacier and crossing into Chile

By Santa Cruz Profunda, from Puerto San Julián · Updated 2026-11-27

Lago del Desierto lies 37 km north of El Chaltén, at the end of a gravel road that follows the Río de las Vueltas. It is the classic outing for a day when clouds hide Fitz Roy, but it is also a destination in its own right: it has boat trips, a glacier less than an hour's walk away, quiet trails and a border crossing you do on foot.

It also has history: there was an armed clash between Argentina and Chile at this lake in 1965, and an arbitration that was only settled in 1995.

Where Lago del Desierto is

It is in the Lago Argentino department, in northwestern Santa Cruz, near the border with Chile. It fills a narrow glacial valley between Lake O'Higgins/San Martín and Fitz Roy, framed by the Vespignani range to the west and the Martínez de Rozas and Del Bosque ranges to the east, according to Wikipedia. It sits at 506 m above sea level and, according to Visit Argentina, covers 8.9 km².

The Río de las Vueltas flows out of it down to Lake Viedma. The lake and the border area make up the Lago del Desierto Provincial Reserve, created by provincial Law 2820 in 2005; it is not part of Los Glaciares National Park. In Chile it is called Laguna del Desierto.

How to get to Lago del Desierto

El Chaltén is the base. From town you head north on Provincial Route 41 (formerly numbered RP 23), a gravel road that follows the Río de las Vueltas to Punta Sur, the southern end of the lake, where the road ends.

By car

It is the most flexible option. At Punta Sur there is a free parking area a few meters from the start of the Huemul glacier trail, according to the El Chaltén portal.

By transfer or tour

In El Chaltén there are private transfers and agencies that go to the lake, with or without the boat trip included. Schedules and prices change every season: ask in town.

From Chile

You can also reach the lake from the north, on foot or by bike, from Candelario Mancilla on Lake O'Higgins/San Martín. That Chilean hamlet is about 30 km south of Villa O'Higgins, according to Wikipedia.

A bit of history

For decades, the area between boundary marker 62 (on the southern shore of Lake O'Higgins/San Martín) and Fitz Roy was disputed between Argentina and Chile. The root cause was how little geographic information about the region existed when the 1881 boundary treaty was signed.

On November 6, 1965, an Argentine Gendarmería patrol clashed with Chilean Carabineros in the forests of the area. Chilean lieutenant Hernán Merino was killed and a sergeant was wounded.

In 1985, as a geopolitical strategy, Argentina founded El Chaltén at the foot of Fitz Roy. On October 21, 1994, an arbitral tribunal ruled in Argentina's favor over 481 km² in dispute, and on October 13, 1995 it rejected Chile's request for reconsideration. That closed the conflict.

What to do at Lago del Desierto

Boat trip from Punta Sur to Punta Norte

The boat crosses the lake end to end in just under an hour, according to the El Chaltén portal. Along the way you see the Vespignani range with its hanging glaciers and the Del Bosque range. At Punta Norte there is the Gendarmería post and a view of the north face of Fitz Roy.

For the current season, the operator Receptivo Chaltén lists departures from Punta Sur at 10 a.m. and 4 p.m., and returns from Punta Norte at 11 a.m. and 5 p.m., from November 1 to March 31, every day except Wednesdays. It notes that the boat may be rescheduled or canceled due to weather. Schedules and fares: check before you travel at Receptivo Chaltén – Cruce internacional por Lago del Desierto.

Huemul glacier trail

It is the shortest and most popular walk. It starts next to the Punta Sur parking area. According to the El Chaltén portal:

  • Distance: 4 km round trip.
  • Duration: about 2 hours in total; less than an hour going up.
  • Difficulty: easy, though with steep sections.
  • Season: September to May.
  • Access: paid, because the whole trail is on private property. The portal lists an entrance fee of USD 11, and children under 10 go free.

The trail climbs through lenga forest to a ridge with views of the Río de las Vueltas valley and Fitz Roy. About 15 more minutes along the ridge take you to the Huemul lagoon, at the foot of the glacier. Current fee: check before you travel at Portal El Chaltén – Glaciar Huemul (Lago del Desierto).

Lake viewpoint

From Punta Sur a trail of about 300 m leads to a viewpoint overlooking the whole lake, according to Página/12.

Trek to Laguna Larga and the border marker

This is the option if you take the boat across and want to hike at Punta Norte. According to the El Chaltén portal:

  • Distance: about 10 km round trip, with about 220 m of elevation gain.
  • Duration: 4 to 5 hours.
  • Difficulty: moderate.
  • Season: October to April.
  • Cost: free.

It starts behind the Gendarmería post, climbs for about half an hour and continues through a quiet, little-visited lenga forest to Laguna Larga and the border marker with Chile. You must carry ID and register with the Gendarmería before setting off. With the morning boat and the afternoon boat you have several hours at Punta Norte; confirm the schedules before booking.

Crossing into Chile on foot

From Punta Norte you can keep walking to Candelario Mancilla. According to Página/12, the route skirts Laguna Larga and Laguna Redonda to the border and continues to Lake O'Higgins/San Martín; it is about six hours of fairly flat walking, and it can be done by bike.

Exit formalities are done with the Gendarmería at Punta Norte, and entry formalities at the Carabineros post in Candelario Mancilla. Ask for the stamp in your passport. According to Wikipedia, photography and drones are prohibited around the border post. From Candelario Mancilla, a boat crosses the lake to Villa O'Higgins: Chilean operators publish days and schedules each season. Step by step and documents: Border crossings from Santa Cruz to Chile and check before you travel at Argentina.gob.ar – Recomendaciones para cruzar a Chile.

Chorrillo del Salto

4 km from El Chaltén, on the same road, is this waterfall, reached by a short walk from the road. More in What to do in El Chaltén besides Fitz Roy.

Kayaking and fishing

The El Chaltén portal lists a kayak outing on Lago del Desierto of about two and a half hours. You can also fish for trout with a provincial permit: according to the 2026/2027 regulations, the season on the lake runs from October 1, 2026 to May 3, 2027, and fishing from a boat is only allowed by oar or with an electric motor. Details in Sport fishing in Santa Cruz: 2026/27 season, permits and where to fish.

When to go

According to the El Chaltén portal, the Huemul glacier trail is done from September to May and the Laguna Larga trek from October to April. The boat and the crossing into Chile run from November 1 to March 31, according to Receptivo Chaltén. In autumn, the ñire trees turn red; in winter, according to Página/12, the road may require 4x4.

The climate is cold and dry, according to Visit Argentina, and the wind can change boat plans. More in Weather in Santa Cruz: when to travel, wind and temperatures.

How many days to stay

Visit Argentina recommends 1 to 2 days.

  • Half a day: Punta Sur viewpoint, Huemul glacier and Chorrillo del Salto.
  • A full day: add the boat trip and the Laguna Larga trek.
  • Two days or more: if you are crossing into Chile, or want to combine the lake with kayaking or fishing.

Travel tips

  • Fill up in El Chaltén before leaving and come back with fuel to spare.
  • Bring warm layers, a windbreaker and a rain jacket, even if you set off in sunshine.
  • Bring water and food if you are hiking at Punta Norte.
  • If you are crossing into Chile, bring a valid passport or ID, check entry requirements in advance and leave spare days in case a boat is canceled.
  • Book the boat trip in advance in January and February.

Frequently asked questions

How many kilometers is it from El Chaltén to Lago del Desierto?

It is 37 km on Provincial Route 41, a gravel road, to Punta Sur.

Do you have to pay to visit the Huemul glacier?

Yes, because the trail is on private property. The El Chaltén portal lists an entrance fee of USD 11, and children under 10 go free; check the current fee before you go.

Can you cross into Chile from Lago del Desierto?

Yes, on foot or by bike, between Punta Norte and Candelario Mancilla. You clear immigration with the Gendarmería and with the Carabineros, and then take a boat to Villa O'Higgins.

Is it worth it if it is cloudy in El Chaltén?

Yes. The forest, the lake and the Huemul glacier are worth it even when Fitz Roy is hidden.
