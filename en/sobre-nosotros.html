About us & contact

Santa Cruz Profunda is an independent guide to the province of Santa Cruz, made in Puerto San Julián. The idea is simple: to cover the whole province of Santa Cruz, from the Andes to the coast, with practical information for traveling by road.

How we work

  • Every fact comes from a source we cite at the bottom of the page (government agencies, national parks, municipalities and, when there's nothing else, established guides and media).
  • Anything that changes often (opening hours, fees, border crossings, road conditions) is linked to the official source so you can confirm it before you set off.
  • We don't publish paid reviews, made-up testimonials or statistics we don't have.
  • The basic directory is free. Businesses can pay for a featured plan for extra visibility, always clearly labeled as such.
