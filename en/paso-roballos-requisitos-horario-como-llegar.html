Home › Articles › Paso Roballos: opening hours, requirements and how to cross from Lago Posadas to Cochrane

By Santa Cruz Profunda, from Puerto San Julián · Updated 2026-11-25

Paso Roballos links northwestern Santa Cruz with Cochrane, in Chile's Aysén Region. On the Argentine side you get there on Provincial Route 41, near Lago Posadas; on the Chilean side, route X-83 runs down the Chacabuco Valley to the Carretera Austral.

It is a gravel crossing, far from everything and with little traffic. That is why you should arrive with your papers ready, a full tank and the crossing's status confirmed on the day. For a comparison with the province's other crossings, see Border crossings from Santa Cruz to Chile.

Hours and season

Argentina's Ministry of Security lists opening hours of 8 am to 8 pm for Roballos, based on data entered by the National Gendarmerie. Wikipedia describes it as permanently authorized, with different hours for summer and winter.

Being authorized does not mean you can cross every day. In winter snow can cut the road: on 23 June 2024, for example, the Provincial Roads Agency reported RP 41 between the RP 103 junction and Paso Rodolfo Roballos as impassable due to snow. That same winter, Aysén's Presidential Delegation recommended crossing the region's border passes in 4x4 vehicles with chains.

Status on the day: check before you travel at Argentina.gob.ar – Pasos internacionales: Paso Roballos (estado y contacto). The road report is published by the Provincial Roads Agency. In our view, the easiest time to cross is summer, from November to March.

Requirements and paperwork

On the Chilean side: Carabineros control

Roballos is not a border complex with the Investigations Police (PDI). According to Aysén's Presidential Delegation, it is one of the region's crossings controlled by Carabineros de Chile. When those crossings reopened in 2022, Carabineros explained that there it takes over the duties of the SAG, Customs, the PDI and health control: it checks what you carry, people's records and the departure of minors.

Safe-conduct to leave Chile

If you return from Chile to Argentina via Roballos, you need a safe-conduct (salvoconducto). ChileAtiende explains that it is the document for leaving Chile through authorized crossings controlled by Carabineros, and that it is not needed at crossings with PDI immigration control.

  • You request it on the PDI website, under the “Salvoconducto” option, and it arrives by email.
  • Chileans, foreign residents and tourists with regular status can request it.
  • You need a valid ID document or passport.
  • It is free and valid only for the departure day you stated.

Get it before you set off: in such remote areas there may be no signal. Up-to-date requirements: check before you travel at ChileAtiende (PDI) – Salvoconducto para viaje al extranjero.

Documents, car and SAG declaration

For the rest of the paperwork (each person's document, the car's papers and the SAG sworn declaration to enter Chile), the same rules apply as at other crossings. They are explained in Border crossings from Santa Cruz to Chile and, step by step, in Paso Jeinimeni: how to cross from Los Antiguos to Chile Chico. Don't carry fresh fruit, meat or dairy: finish them before crossing.

Getting there

From Los Antiguos on Route 41

RP 41 is the scenic gravel road between Los Antiguos and Lago Posadas. According to the province, its highest point is El Portezuelo, at 1,500 m, with views of Cerro Colorado and Cerro Zeballos. From there the road descends toward the Roballos area and, in good weather, you can see Cerro San Lorenzo, 3,706 m high.

At the Río Ghio bridge the road forks: one branch goes to the Chile crossing at Paso Roballos and the other to Lago Posadas. The full route, with viewpoints and audio guide, is in Lago Posadas and Route 41.

From Route 40, via Bajo Caracoles

If you are coming along Route 40, turn off at Bajo Caracoles toward Lago Posadas and, from the village area, continue north on RP 41 to the border.

On the Chilean side: Chacabuco Valley and Cochrane

After the border, route X-83 crosses the Chacabuco Valley, inside Chile's Patagonia National Park. Ruta0 estimates about 86 km of gravel between the crossing and Cochrane. According to CONAF, the valley road meets the Carretera Austral 17 km before Cochrane.

What to see on the other side

The Chacabuco Valley is part of Patagonia National Park, managed in Chile by CONAF. It has trails such as La Confluencia (3 km, easy) and Cañadón Chacabuco (10 km, easy), plus camping areas. For the 2026-2027 season, CONAF reports West Wind open from 1 October, Casa Piedra from 15 October and Alto Valle from 1 November 2026. Some trails may have snow in spring: check with the rangers before heading out.

On the Argentine side, the same park continues as Patagonia National Park in Santa Cruz, with the La Ascensión portal on RP 41.

Travel tips

  • Fuel: leave Los Antiguos or Perito Moreno with a full tank. According to Turismo Ruta 40, there is fuel in Lago Posadas and Bajo Caracoles, but not always. On the Chilean side, PasosHoy places the nearest station in Cochrane, using data from Bencina en Línea.
  • Check the status on the day and arrive well before 8 pm: formalities take time.
  • Vehicle: it is a mountain gravel road. Drive slowly, check your spare tire and, outside summer, carry chains.
  • In case there is no signal: download maps, the safe-conduct and the Route 41 audio guide before you leave.
  • Water, food and warm clothes: there are no services along the way.

To continue your trip: Perito Moreno, the Cueva de las Manos and Perito Moreno National Park.

Frequently asked questions

What are the opening hours of Paso Roballos?

8 am to 8 pm, according to Argentina's Ministry of Security. Conditions change with the weather: check on the day on the official border page or by phone at (2963) 432033.

Do I need a safe-conduct to cross at Roballos?

To leave Chile, yes. On the Chilean side the crossing is run by Carabineros, and ChileAtiende says the PDI safe-conduct is required at those crossings. It is free, requested online and valid only for the stated departure day.

Is Paso Roballos open in winter?

It is authorized year-round, but snow can cut RP 41: in June 2024 the Provincial Roads Agency reported it impassable up to the border. In winter, check the road report and carry chains.

How far is it from Paso Roballos to Cochrane?

About 86 km of gravel according to Ruta0, on route X-83 through the Chacabuco Valley, with the last 17 km on the Carretera Austral.
