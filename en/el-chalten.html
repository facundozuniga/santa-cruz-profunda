Home › Destinations › El Chaltén

El Chaltén is the “National Trekking Capital”: the province counts more than fifty trails. The landscape is dominated by Mount Fitz Roy (or Chaltén) and Cerro Torre.

Classic trails and spots

  • Mirador de Los Cóndores (Condor lookout).
  • Laguna de los Tres, at the foot of Fitz Roy.
  • Laguna Torre.
  • Loma del Pliegue Tumbado.
  • Chorrillo del Salto.
  • Piedra del Fraile.

Also: horseback riding, sport fishing and rafting. Trail conditions and camping rules are set by Los Glaciares National Park: check before you travel at Turismo Santa Cruz – El Chaltén.

Getting there

Fly into El Calafate (FTE) and from there it's about 220 km by scheduled bus or car. Tourism office: Perito Moreno 28.

Traveling on? Back on Route 40, Tres Lagos is the next fuel stop heading north.
