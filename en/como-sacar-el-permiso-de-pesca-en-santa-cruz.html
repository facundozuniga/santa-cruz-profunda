Home › Articles › How to get a fishing permit in Santa Cruz (fishing license)

By Santa Cruz Profunda, from Puerto San Julián · Updated 2026-11-23

To fish in rivers, lakes and streams in Santa Cruz you need a provincial permit, also called a fishing license. The process is online, on the SIPESQ system at pesca.santacruz.gob.ar, and if you can’t do it online there is in-person help in Río Gallegos.

The form is not complicated, but it helps to know a few things before you start: which category applies to you, which documents you need to upload and how to pay. This guide is the step-by-step walkthrough. If you are looking for river-by-river dates, bag limits and where to fish, see our general guide Sport fishing in Santa Cruz: 2026/27 season, permits and where to fish.

Who needs a permit

Everyone who fishes in the province’s inland waters. The regulations require you to always carry the permit and an ID. Some categories are free, but you still have to apply for them:

  • Children up to 12 (a parent or guardian applies).
  • People over 65.
  • Retirees and pensioners.
  • People with disabilities, with official certification.
  • Falklands/Malvinas War veterans, with official certification: their permit is permanent.

The permit is personal, issued in your name and non-transferable: you can’t lend it.

Which permit to choose

On the form each category appears with a code. These are the 2026/2027 season prices, set by Disposición N.º 242/2026 and published on the portal. They may be updated, so check them before paying: check before you travel at Pesca Santa Cruz (provincia) – Valores y categorías 2026/2027.

Trolling: if you will fish by trolling from a boat, you also need an additional permit (AT-D $20,000 per day, AT-S $30,000 per week or AT-T $40,000 per season). It does not replace the regular permit: you need both.

Which one suits me?

  • Going for a day or two: daily or weekend.
  • Going for a week: the weekly permit costs less than two daily ones.
  • Coming back several times in the season: the season permit.
  • Member of a club with current legal status: the SC-T costs half the regular season permit.

Before you start: what to have on hand

  • Your ID (the form accepts a DNI, passport, enrolment booklet, civic booklet or identity card), photographed or scanned front and back.
  • An email address you check.
  • The payment receipt, if your permit has a cost.
  • If applicable: retiree or pensioner card, Certificado Único de Discapacidad (CUD), veteran’s certificate or proof of up-to-date fishing club dues.

Files are uploaded as PDF, JPEG, PNG or TIFF.

The online process, step by step

Go to pesca.santacruz.gob.ar and click “Solicitar Permiso”. The form has four screens, plus a final confirmation: check before you travel at Pesca Santa Cruz (provincia) – Solicitar permiso de pesca.

1. Personal details

Surname and first name, document type and number (no dots or spaces), date of birth, address, phone and email. If you live outside Argentina, tick the “No soy residente del país” (I am not a resident) box.

2. Permit type

Indicate your employment status (working, retired, pensioner or other), whether you are a resident and whether you belong to a fishing club. Then choose the category and the start date. The form also asks for your destination town or area.

3. Documents

Upload your ID, front and back. If you are applying for a special category, add the supporting document: retiree or pensioner card, CUD, veteran’s certificate or club receipt.

4. Payment

The form offers bank deposit or bank transfer, always in pesos: transfers in foreign currency are not accepted. The account details (CBU and CUIT) appear on the form itself; copy them from there and not from other websites. If you pay with Mercado Pago, the receipt must show the full name of the account holder who paid. Then enter the date, the transaction number and the receipt.

5. Confirm and submit

Accept the terms of the regulations and submit. The form also includes a voluntary authorization for inspectors to check your backpack if they ask during an inspection.

After submitting: can I fish yet?

Yes. The official form states that the permit is valid for fishing from the moment it is created, while the application remains pending administrative validation. As the Dirección de Pesca Continental explained at the start of the season, you will receive a verification email with a note that allows you to fish while the review is completed; you don’t need to enter your details again. Keep that email on your phone. If false documents are detected, fines or penalties may apply.

To see the status of your application, use the “Consultar Permiso” option on the portal.

If you can’t do it online

Staff at the Secretaría de Estado de Pesca y Acuicultura help you complete the application at Avellaneda 801, Río Gallegos, Monday to Friday from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., as the office announced at the start of the 2026/2027 season (Noticias D). For enquiries:

  • Dirección de Pesca Continental: +54 2966 429462, Monday to Friday from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. according to the portal, or pesca-continental@santacruz.gob.ar.
  • Technical problems with the portal: pesca@santacruz.gob.ar.

More contact details, including fishing wardens and emergencies: check before you travel at Pesca Santa Cruz (provincia) – Datos de contacto.

What to carry when you go fishing

  • The permit, on paper or on your phone. It does not need a stamp.
  • Your ID.

You show both to any enforcement officer who asks: inspectors from the Dirección de Pesca Continental, the Provincial Police, the Coast Guard (Prefectura) or the Gendarmería. You can also ask enforcement staff for their ID; the portal publishes the list of inspectors.

Validity and season

  • 2026/2027 general season: from 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027.
  • Season permits: valid until 30 September 2027.
  • Santa Cruz only: the permit is valid within the province and for the dates shown on it.
  • Each water has its own dates: for example, the Gallegos River is open from 1 November 2026 to 30 May 2027. Having a permit doesn’t mean every river is open: check Annex I of the regulations.

Three rules to know before your first cast

  • Mandatory catch and release in rivers. In all rivers and streams, and within 200 m of their sources and their mouths into lakes, release is immediate, mandatory and harmless (except for Annex I exceptions). There you use a single single or double hook, micro-barbed or with the barb flattened, and you may not keep or transport any fish. To handle and release fish properly, follow the recommendations in Annex II of the regulations: check before you travel at Pesca Santa Cruz (provincia) – Reglamento General de Pesca Deportiva 2026/2027.
  • Daylight only. The fishing day runs from sunrise to sunset; night fishing is prohibited.
  • Didymo. When you move between waters, remove any algae and mud, soak your gear in bleach solution or water hotter than 60 °C, and let it dry in the sun for 48 hours.

Fishing without a permit carries a fine of ten modules, equal to ten season permits: $400,000 according to the portal. The rest of the rules, bag limits and waters are in our guide to sport fishing in Santa Cruz.

What about national parks?

Inside national parks, such as the Los Glaciares National Park sector, the provincial permit is not enough: National Parks regulations apply and you need their permit. We explain it in Sport fishing in Santa Cruz.

Frequently asked questions

Can I fish as soon as I submit the form?

Yes. According to the official form, the permit is valid for fishing from the moment it is created while it is being validated. Keep the verification email on your phone.

Do I have to print the permit?

No. You can carry it on paper or on your phone, along with your ID. It does not need a stamp.

Do children need a permit?

Yes. Up to age 12 it is free, and from 13 to 17 it costs $15,000 for the season. In both cases a parent or guardian applies, signs and is responsible for any of the minor’s violations.

I am retired: do I have to pay?

No, but you still have to apply and upload your retiree or pensioner card.

Can a foreign visitor pay in dollars?

No. The portal does not accept transfers in foreign currency: payment is in Argentine pesos.

Is a permit from another province valid?

No. In Santa Cruz you fish with the provincial permit issued through SIPESQ, and that permit is in turn valid only within Santa Cruz.

Can I fish at night?

No. Only between sunrise and sunset.

Who do I contact if I have a problem with the application?

At pesca-continental@santacruz.gob.ar or +54 2966 429462, Monday to Friday from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. For technical problems with the portal, pesca@santacruz.gob.ar.
