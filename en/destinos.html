North coast · Deseado estuary

Puerto Deseado

A 40 km estuary carved into the plateau, with dolphins and cormorants minutes from the harbor and rockhopper penguins out at sea.

Photo: Ulises Icardi · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

North · San Jorge Gulf

Caleta Olivia

The northern gateway to the province: cliffs, a 4.5 km coastal promenade and the Gorosito, the monument to the oil worker.

Photo: Daniel Alegre · Public domain · Wikimedia Commons

North · Deseado Massif

Pico Truncado

Argentina's National Hydrogen Capital and the gateway to the MARA Route, an open-air museum in the Deseado Massif.

Photo: josema carambia · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

North · Steppe

Las Heras

The last station of the Patagonian Railway, deep in the steppe and surrounded by canyons.

Photo: Mikelzubi · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

North · Route 3

Jaramillo and Fitz Roy

Two former Patagonian Railway stations, 19 km apart: stone houses, Ice Age fossils and the story of Facón Grande.

Photo: rodoluca · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

North · Marine park

Penguin Island and the Deseado Estuary

Rockhopper penguins, sea lions and elephant seals off Puerto Deseado, reachable only by sea.

Photo: Mikelzubi · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

North · Coast

Cabo Blanco Nature Reserve

A lighthouse on white rocks, a South American fur seal breeding colony and an abandoned village.

Photo: Gonce · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

North · RP 43

Koluel Kayke

A village born from a Patagonian Railway station, on the road that crosses the province from east to west.

Central plateau

Petrified forests, fishing lakes and the history of 1921.

Photo: Eassi · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Central plateau · RP 49

Jaramillo Petrified Forests

Giant fossil trunks, relatives of the monkey-puzzle tree, lying on the open plateau since the Jurassic.

Photo: Hiroki Ogawa · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Central plateau · Route 40

Gobernador Gregores and Lake Cardiel

The town in the Río Chico valley: world-class fishing at Cardiel and Strobel lakes, the history of 1921 and the gateway to Perito Moreno National Park.

Atlantic coast (Route 3)

Bays, penguin colonies and historic towns, from San Julián to Río Gallegos.

Photo: Gastón Cuello · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Coast · Route 3

Puerto San Julián

The bay where Magellan's expedition spent the winter of 1520 — today a small town on Route 3, with marine wildlife just minutes from the center.

Photo: Mikelzubi · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Coast · Route 3 km 2400

Monte León National Park

36 km of cliffs and beaches, a penguin colony 40 minutes' walk away and a peninsula shaped like a lion lying down.

Photo: Carlos Zito · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Coast · Santa Cruz River estuary

Puerto Santa Cruz

The province's first capital, on the wide Santa Cruz River estuary, minutes from Monte León.

Photo: James Cadwell · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Coast · Santa Cruz River

Comandante Luis Piedra Buena

An oasis on the Santa Cruz River: Isla Pavón, murals, Patoruzito and steelhead fishing.

Andes & Route 40

Lakes, rock art, glaciers and trekking from the northwest to the southwest.

Photo: Claudio Elias · Public domain · Wikimedia Commons

Northwest · Lake Buenos Aires

Los Antiguos

Cherry orchards on the shores of Lake Buenos Aires, a stone's throw from Chile. A rare microclimate in the middle of the steppe.

Photo: Mariano · Public domain · Wikimedia Commons

Route 40 · Río Pinturas

Cueva de las Manos

Negative hand stencils, guanacos and hunting scenes painted in the Río Pinturas canyon. A World Heritage Site since 1999.

Photo: LuigiStudio · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Route 40 · Cañadón Pinturas · La Ascensión

Parque Patagonia

Steppe, canyons and wildlife: pumas, guanacos and lesser rheas. A new park that combines a National Park with portals run by a foundation.

Photo: Ulises Icardi · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Route 40 · Archaeological Capital

Perito Moreno

The base town of the northwest: from here you head out to the Cueva de las Manos, Parque Patagonia and Los Antiguos.

Photo: Ppviorgullo · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Route 40 · Lakes Posadas and Pueyrredón

Lago Posadas

Two lakes of different colors separated by an isthmus, rock art and one of the smallest, most remote villages in the province.

Photo: Fernando de Gorocica · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Andes · Lake Argentino

El Calafate

The star of Patagonian tourism, on Lake Argentino and the gateway to the Perito Moreno Glacier.

Photo: Gedankenstuecke · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Andes · National Trekking Capital

El Chaltén

More than fifty trekking routes at the foot of Fitz Roy and Cerro Torre.

Photo: alvaro errandonea · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Andes · Route 40

Tres Lagos

A rural village at the junction for lakes Viedma, Tar and San Martín, with Piedra Clavada and a petrified forest.

Photo: Florenciabrayer · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Andes · North of El Chaltén

Lago del Desierto

A long lake among forests, with Fitz Roy in the background, the Huemul Glacier and the crossing to Chile on foot.

Photo: Ulises Icardi · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Andes · Route 40

Bajo Caracoles

The most famous hamlet on Route 40: a fuel pump in the steppe and the junction for the Cueva de las Manos and Lago Posadas.

The provincial capital, Cabo Vírgenes and the mining towns on the Chilean border.

South · Provincial capital

Río Gallegos

The provincial capital: an estuary with huge tides, museums, Arctic birds and the road to Cabo Vírgenes, where Route 40 begins.

Photo: rodoluca · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

South · Coal basin

Río Turbio

Argentina's National Coal Capital, right on the Chilean border, with snow in winter and 30 km from Puerto Natales.

Photo: GsusESC · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

South · Coal basin

28 de Noviembre

The other town in the coal basin: condors, forests and fishing at Puente Blanco.

Photo: Ulises Icardi · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

South · Provincial reserve

Cabo Vírgenes and km 0 of Route 40

Where Route 40 begins: a lighthouse at the mouth of the Strait of Magellan and one of the largest penguin colonies on the continent.

Photo: Osarsan · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

South · Geological reserve

Laguna Azul

An intensely blue lagoon at the bottom of a volcanic crater, an hour from the capital.
